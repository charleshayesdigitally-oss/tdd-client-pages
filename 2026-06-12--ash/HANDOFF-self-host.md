Hey — your page is finished and you're holding the keys. This guide gets it live in about 5 minutes, even if you've never touched a website file in your life.

## What you received

A zip file. Inside it: one folder with your whole page — the design, your story, your photos, everything. **Keep the folder together.** The page and its photos travel as a set; if you move the page file out by itself, the photos won't load.

Your buy buttons already point at your checkout link (the one you gave me in the intake). Nothing to wire up.

## Option 1 — Netlify Drop (free, ~5 minutes, what I recommend)

Netlify is a hosting service with a free tier that's perfect for this. No code, no credit card.

1. Unzip the file you received. You'll see a folder — remember where it is.
2. Go to **app.netlify.com/drop**
3. It will ask you to create a free account (email or Google login). Do that — it's how you keep control of your page later.
4. You'll see a box that says "Drag and drop your site folder here." **Drag the unzipped folder into it.**
5. Wait about 30 seconds. Netlify gives you a link — something like `wonderful-name-123.netlify.app`. That's your live page. Click it. That's real. You can send it to anyone right now.

**Want a prettier link?** In Netlify: Site settings → Change site name. Now it's `yourbrand.netlify.app`.

**Want it on your own domain** (like `pages.yourbrand.com`)? Netlify walks you through it under Domain settings — or reply to your delivery email and I'll help as an add-on.

## Option 2 — You already have a website

If you have hosting (WordPress, cPanel, anything with a file manager): upload the whole folder to your host, usually into a subfolder like `/launch/`. Your page lives at `yourdomain.com/launch/`. If those words meant nothing to you, use Option 1 — it's genuinely the easier road.

## "Can I put this on my Stan Store / Beacons?"

Honest answer: no — and that's by design, not a limitation of your page. Stan and Beacons don't accept custom pages; their editors only build their own blocks.

Here's the move instead, and it's better: your page lives at its own link (Option 1), and your **buy button already points into your Stan checkout.** Stan keeps handling the money, delivery, all of it. Your new page just does the convincing first.

Put your page link:
- in your bio (instead of the raw Stan link)
- in your stories and DMs when someone asks "what do you do?"
- anywhere you'd have sent people to your store

Stan is your cash register. This page is your storefront window.

## Making changes yourself later

It's your file — you're allowed to touch it. Here's the honest map of what's safe:

**Easy (go for it):**
- **Changing words.** Open `index.html` in any text editor (Notepad works). Press Ctrl+F, type the sentence you want to change, edit the words, save. Re-drag your folder into Netlify and the change is live. Don't delete anything that looks like `<this>` — those are the page's bones.
- **Swapping a photo.** Put your new image in the `photos` folder with the exact same filename as the one it replaces. Re-upload. Done.
- **The shortcut:** paste the whole file into ChatGPT or Claude and say "change my price to $37" — it'll hand back the edited file. Genuinely works.

**Not worth risking on your own:** adding sections, changing the layout, restyling. The page is built as one connected piece, and the order of the sections is doing real selling work — it isn't decoration. For anything structural, reply to your delivery email instead. Small jobs get quoted small.
