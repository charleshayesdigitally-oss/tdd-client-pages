Hey Alison — your page has been rebuilt to our new design standard. **Same offer, same words, same checkout and links — just a big level-up on the look and feel.** Getting it live is the same move you made the first time:

## Steps (about 5 minutes)
1. **Download** the zip (`download.zip`).
2. **Unzip it.** You'll get a folder with `index.html` and your `photos`. Keep them together — the page and photos travel as a set.
3. **Re-deploy it the same way you put up the first one:**
   - **If you're on Netlify:** open your AiLisia site → **Deploys** → drag the unzipped folder onto the deploy area. It replaces the old version; `ailisialabs.com` updates in about 30 seconds.
   - **If you uploaded to your own host:** upload the folder's contents over the existing files.
4. **Hard-refresh** `ailisialabs.com` (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R) and you'll see the new design.

That's it. Your buy buttons and links are exactly as they were — nothing to re-wire.
