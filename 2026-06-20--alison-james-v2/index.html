Why this exists

I spent years managing millions of dollars for other people.

In institutional finance, I understood at a structural level how money moves, how systems work, how capital gets protected and deployed at scale. I knew this not just in theory. It was my job.

Meanwhile, people I loved, six-figure earners, highly credentialed professionals, were working rideshare apps and second jobs to cover basic expenses. Not because they didn’t earn enough. Because nobody ever gave them a system.

Then it got personal. My dog needed emergency surgery. I borrowed money from a friend. And when payday came, my boss told me he didn’t know when he could pay us.

“That Friday at lunch I walked out, turned to my coworker, and said ‘I’m going home to figure out the rest of my life, because this ain’t it.’ I never went back.”

That moment didn’t break me. It clarified everything. I had the institutional knowledge. I had lived the collapse. And I rebuilt.
